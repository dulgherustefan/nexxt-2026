# Raiffeisen Bank România and RBI: business profile, how operations are organised, AI and automation (2024–2026), and the NEXXT Summit 2026 / VibeHack context

Research date: 28 Oct 2026, the first day of the hackathon. Romanian names and terms are kept in Romanian. Executive statements are paraphrased in English, with one short verbatim quote. ## 1. Raiffeisen Bank România key figures (2024–2026), recent strategic moves and notable news

### Takeaway
Raiffeisen Bank România (RBRO) is a highly profitable, mid-sized bank. In 2025 it made a net profit of 1.75 bn lei, held about 88.6 bn lei in assets, served about 2.33 million clients and had a cost/income ratio of 42.6%, a historic low. By assets it ranks 6th in Romania, with about a 9.3% share. Its strategy combines heavy digitalisation, a shrinking headcount (down about 5% in 2025, which the bank attributes mainly to digitalisation projects) and an acquisition: it agreed to buy Garanti BBVA România for about €591m, with closing expected in Q4 2026 and integration through H2 2027.

### Inferences
- Romanian peers in the 2026 press report higher cost/income ratios, for example BRD at 49.6% in H1 2026 (see section 6). Raiffeisen's 42.6% suggests it is already efficient. The jury will likely want incremental, measurable gains (minutes saved per case, error rates, SLA) rather than broad "transformation" claims. Comparing these figures is only indicative, because the periods and methods differ.
- Reported H1 2026 net profit (849m lei) is below H1 2025 (896m lei), roughly −5%, even though volumes grew strongly and opex rose 5%. This is my comparison of the two press figures and may involve different perimeters. Cost pressure makes an operational-efficiency pitch timely.
- The Garanti BBVA integration (Q4 2026 to H2 2027) will create one-off, high-volume operational work: client and data migration, KYC remediation for incoming clients, reconciliation between legacy and target systems, and document re-validation. This is a concrete hook for the hackathon directions "reconcile records" and "validate applications".
- Headcount fell about 5% while clients grew 22% and more. The bank explicitly links the headcount reduction to digitalisation, so automation that absorbs volume growth without new hiring fits the stated direction.

### Gaps
- No public 2025 figure for total "active clients" was found, apart from digital-client and Smart Mobile metrics.
- A search summary attributed a +8% 2025 profit-growth figure to Wall-Street.ro. It could not be verified and conflicts with the +4% reported by AGERPRES, Economica.net and BankingNews. Use +4%.
- Garanti BBVA România's own figures (clients, employees, branches) were not in the press release.
- No absolute cost/income or operating income figures for H1 2026 were published.

## 2. Automation Raiffeisen România already uses (RPA, AI, genAI, chatbots, document processing, partnerships) and RBI group AI programmes

### Takeaway
Raiffeisen România has had an in-house AI department since 2019. In 2024 it had about 30 ML/MLOps/DevOps/data engineers and 12 AI applications. Its best-known internal tool is **ARI**, an assistant over internal procedures and product knowledge launched in 2024, and it reportedly runs an agentic platform called "ARIA". Public sources also show: RPA plus automatic document reading in SME account opening (2025), OCR in HR pre-boarding, a 24/7 chatbot "Ana" for SMEs, an AI coach "ERIC" for entrepreneurs, digital onboarding with video selfie, and the Medallia-based PULS feedback platform. At group level, RBI reports more than 160 live AI use cases, an internal GPT on Azure OpenAI with over 20,000 users, an AI Hub with agentic pipelines, and over 1,700 "AI Pioneers". RBI's shared service centre CRISP runs group reconciliations on Broadridge. I found no public UiPath case study for Raiffeisen România.

### Cited Findings
**AI department and ARI (Artificial Raiffeisen Intelligence)**
- The AI department was founded in 2019. It has about 30 experts (ML Engineers, ML Ops, DevOps, Data Engineers) and had built 12 applications using AI models by June 2024 — [raiffeisen.ro press release, 13 Jun 2024](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/ro/despre-noi/comunicate-de-presa/2024/raiffeisen-bank-romania-dezvolta-ari-un-instrument-inovator-de-artificial-intelligence.html).
- ARI is an AI learning tool and virtual assistant for employees, designed by the team in Romania. It gives quick access to specialised information and supports onboarding and continuous training. Before launch, over 200 bank specialists trained it through more than 5,000 interactions in Personal Financial Planning. Its domains include mortgage lending (documentation, refinancing, loan insurance, guarantees, property eligibility), savings, insurance, investment funds, pensions, internal procedures and banking regulations. The bank reports that it answered over 70% of the questions asked, with a 98% correct-answer rate — [raiffeisen.ro press release](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/ro/despre-noi/comunicate-de-presa/2024/raiffeisen-bank-romania-dezvolta-ari-un-instrument-inovator-de-artificial-intelligence.html). The press release does not name the LLM, cloud or RAG approach — [same](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/ro/despre-noi/comunicate-de-presa/2024/raiffeisen-bank-romania-dezvolta-ari-un-instrument-inovator-de-artificial-intelligence.html).
- A press roundup describes ARI as trained by 200 specialists and built to raise team productivity and learning speed — [Profit.ro](https://www.profit.ro/povesti-cu-profit/financiar/banci/raiffeisen-lanseaza-ari-instrument-ai-conceput-local-si-antrenat-pana-acum-de-200-de-specialisti-ai-bancii-pentru-a-creste-productivitatea-echipelor-si-viteza-de-invatare-21628530) (search snippet). **RPA and document processing (2025)**
- In 2025, the SME/Antreprenori division accelerated AI-based technologies and advanced process automation (RPA). The aims were to cut response times for SME account opening, raise operational accuracy and simplify client experience. **Automatic document-reading solutions (*soluții de citire automată a documentelor*)** reduce manual interventions in operational flows and the risk of human error — [Raport anual 2025, "Optimizare și automatizare", approx. p. 43](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/content/dam/rbi/retail/eu/ro/documents/rapoarte/anuale/2025-Raport-anual-Raiffeisen-Bank.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf).
- The Digital Advisory Tool (DAT) is used by over 80% of SME advisers. It centralises information and **generates offers automatically**, and was used for about 21,500 existing and potential clients in 2025. Some SMEs complete contracting in under 15 minutes. The 2026 plan is to extend AI use in analysis and offer generation (*analiză și ofertare*) and to fully digitalise acquisition — [Raport anual 2025, approx. p. 43–44](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/content/dam/rbi/retail/eu/ro/documents/rapoarte/anuale/2025-Raport-anual-Raiffeisen-Bank.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf).
- HR uses an OCR tool that reads employment documents and fills the HRIS during pre-boarding. The applicant tracking system generates job offers automatically, employment contracts are signed digitally, and applications flow in automatically from LinkedIn — [Raport anual 2025, HR "Sistemul de recrutare", approx. p. 30](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/content/dam/rbi/retail/eu/ro/documents/rapoarte/anuale/2025-Raport-anual-Raiffeisen-Bank.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf).
- Health and safety (SSM) was digitalised, including electronic processing of fitness certificates and digital accident management — [Raport anual 2025, HR section](https://www.raiffeisen.ro/content/dam/rbi/retail/eu/ro/documents/rapoarte/anuale/2025-Raport-anual-Raiffeisen-Bank.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf).
- No UiPath or other RPA-vendor case study naming Raiffeisen România was found. The closest item is a 2020 robot from Future WorkForce, a UiPath partner, for loan-deferral requests. It handled up to 240 requests per hour against 20 minutes of manual work per request, but the client named is OTP Bank, not Raiffeisen — [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/Future-WorkForce-robots-banks-ro) (search snippet).

- "Effective and responsible use of AI" is an explicit criterion and echoes the CEO's public stance.
- €8k cash at €2k per winning team implies about 4 winning teams. This is my inference, and allocation across challenges is not published.
- 2024–2025 finalists clustered on client-facing ideas (assistants, onboarding, branch network, fraud). An internal back-office agent (reconciliation, validation, triage) would stand out against those precedents while fitting the "operational efficiency" track.

## 6. What Romanian competitors (BT, BCR/Erste, ING, BRD, CEC) do with AI in operations

### Takeaway
Banca Transilvania is the most visibly advanced. It runs a Druid-based Chat BT that has moved towards agentic AI in BT Pay, an AI agent ("DAVID") for its operations helpdesk, Copilot rollouts, an "AIvolution" employee programme and the first AI-agent-initiated payment in Romania with Visa (Jul 2026), on top of RPA dating to 2018. BRD's parent Société Générale targets up to €600m of AI savings, and BRD is cutting staff costs. ING and CEC talk about AI for internal processes and decision support with strong governance. ### Inferences
- Positioning:
  - BT leads visibly on conversational and agentic AI, especially client-facing and the ops helpdesk.
  - BCR leads on its long automation history.
  - BRD and SG lead on explicit AI cost-savings targets.
  - Raiffeisen's public story centres on in-house AI (ARI/ARIA, the AI department since 2019), digital onboarding, SME RPA and document reading, and responsible human-in-the-loop AI. It has fewer public back-office agent showcases.
- A hackathon agent that makes Raiffeisen's back office measurably faster while keeping a human decision-maker fills a visible gap relative to peers and matches the CEO's framing. For example: reconciliation exception triage at CRISP scale, application or document validation for SME, credit and Garanti migration, or complaint and card-dispute triage.
- Competitors' figures are mostly vendor or self-reported. Avoid quoting them as benchmarks in a pitch without caveats.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-vendor) figures were found on robots, FTE savings or processes automated for BT, BCR, ING, BRD or CEC in 2025–2026.
- BRD-specific operational AI agents and CEC deployments were not described concretely in sources found.
- The dates of several BCR items (13 digital labs, 80% digitalised) could not be verified. BCR's virtual assistant ADA, reportedly built on Druid's conversational platform, came up in search summaries, but the source page was not identified.
